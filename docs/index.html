Cricle is a free daily cricket guessing game, like Wordle for cricket fans. Every day there's a new international cricketer to find in ten guesses. Each guess shows their country, role, batting and bowling style, debut year and matches played: green is a match, orange is close, and arrows point to the answer. Play the daily game, warm up with practice rounds and climb the leaderboard.

Every men's Test, ODI and T20I player for the twelve Test nations, plus ODI players from 13 associate nations.

Player data from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0), Wikidata (CC0) and Cricsheet.
